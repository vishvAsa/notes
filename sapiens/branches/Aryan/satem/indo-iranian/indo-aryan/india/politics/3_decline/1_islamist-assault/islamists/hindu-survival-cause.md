The difference (between other pagan losses vs India) is actually two-fold:

1) The Indian Subcontinent was very populous, so extermination or forced-conversion of all non-Muslims was impractical.

2) The Ḥanafī school of Fiqh in Islam permits jizyah from groups other than the People of the Book (Christians, Jews,  and"Sabians" [Mandaeans?])

In contrast, Europe was less densely populated and Christian polities made paganism, heresy (Donatism, Montanism, Arianism, et al.), Samaritanism, Manichaeanism, etc. illegal. Even Rabbinical Judaism was only reluctantly tolerated.

Unlike pagans who were subjected to the "vengeful sword," the Jews were allowed to survive but not thrive.
