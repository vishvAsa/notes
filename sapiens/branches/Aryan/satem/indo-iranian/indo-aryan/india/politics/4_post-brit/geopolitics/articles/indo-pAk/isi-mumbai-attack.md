Some anti-state elements of Jaish-e-Mohammed splintered off following 9/11, but the pro-Pakistan elements were re-sheltered by the Pakistani state to refocus their attention towards India.

HuJI had a near-total rupture with the Pakistani state following the Lal Masjid siege in 2007.

LeT never had a rupture with the ISI. It's actually regarded as the ISI's most reliable proxy.

The ISI has occasionally forced LeT to temporarily close camps, but these are tactical "cooling periods" due to international pressure.

The ISI had full knowledge of the granular details of 26/11.

David Coleman Headley even testified before a Chicago court that he answered to an ISI officer known as Major Iqbal who provided him with money, communication equipment, and instructions regarding reconnaissance in Mumbai starting in 2006.

It was Major Iqbal who selected the Taj Mahal Palace Hotel, the Oberoi Trident, and Chabad House as targets.

Ashfaq Parvez Kayani (i.e. the Army Chief during the execution phase) may not have known the minutiae, but he was certainly aware of the attacks.
Kayani was DG-ISI from 2004 to 2007.
