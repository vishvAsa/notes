The copper hoard swords

The Cu-hoard/Ocher Colored Pottery culture is characterized by a large number of swords. The mainstream archaeological temporal window for the Cu-hoard/OCP sword production has been listed as 2200-1650 BCE. There is no evidence for true swords (not knives, possible daggers or large spear-heads) in the Harappan sites. The sword is (almost?) entirely absent R^igveda. 

Mainstream indology holds that the Indo-Aryans invaded India after the collapse of mature Harappan civilization, towards the late OCP period, and settled down in the Northwest and North to compose the RV. 

The Painted Greyware (PGW) is unambiguously associated with the Aryans, and there are rare reports of swords associated with PGW sites. 

Taken together, these observations raise a further problem for the widely accepted mainstream hypothesis of the late composition of the RV in India.
