Biṣnupriyā-s are attested the earliest in the 1500s in Kangleipak, and culturally share the most with Meitei, then the Asamīyā and Bāṅgālī-s.

 They migrated out during the Burmese invasion. None of them are around Loktak right now. They settled in areas close to Loktak but not at Loktak. The present  fishing community who live in Loktak are Meitei people.
