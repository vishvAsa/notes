## Beed
One of the greatest H mathematician-scientist bhAskara-II says he was "sahya-kulAchalAshrita-vijjaDaviDa-nagara-nivAsI."
The town is today the provincial town of Beed in mahArAShTra state. There is a little chAlukya age temple in the town, but otherwise desolate from marUnmatta action. From today's state, who would imagine that a man who brought such glory to his people, the very pinnacle of their intellectual expression, would have adorned a town such as this?

What was the kulAchala sahya that he talks about? It is likely the hills about 8 km to south of the town.

## changadeva inscription
inscription of cha~Ngadeva, grandson of bhAskara-II recorded by paNDita rAMasubrahmanyan and colleagues. It records the establishment of a college of astronomy & mathematics in the vicinity of Chalisgaon in Maharashtra. The college was destroyed without trace by the marUnmatta-s
