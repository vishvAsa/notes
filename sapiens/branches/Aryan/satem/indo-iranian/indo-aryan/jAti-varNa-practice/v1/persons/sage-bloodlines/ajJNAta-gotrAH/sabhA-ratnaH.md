Śivaśrī-Sabhāratna-Śivācārya, an Ādiśaiva Vidvān, attained Śivasāyujya on 3 August 2026. 

A glory to the Śivaviprakula, he was conversant with not just Saṃskṛta, Tamil and English but also French and also studied Śāstras in the traditional way. 

One of his most contributions to the Siddhānta was that he translated into English multiple Āgamas:

1. Kāmikāgama—Kriyāpāda—Pūrva & Uttarabhāgas
2. Raurava (Vidyāpāda/VP, partial)
3. Mātaṅga (VP, partial)
4. Mṛgendra
5. Pauṣkara (VP, partial)
6. Sarvajñānottara (VP, Yogapāda/YP)
7. Kiraṇa (VP, YP, Kriyā & Carya pādas)

He made all of the above freely available online through his collaborative effort with Himalayan Academy. 

Many of us were first exposed to the Siddhānta and got to appreciate the beauty of the Sampradāya thanks to his translations. 

May Śiva bless us with everlasting faith in the Siddhānta through His now abhivyakta-sāmya with Śivībhūta Ācārya and may He give Ācārya’s family His anugraha to bear this loss.
