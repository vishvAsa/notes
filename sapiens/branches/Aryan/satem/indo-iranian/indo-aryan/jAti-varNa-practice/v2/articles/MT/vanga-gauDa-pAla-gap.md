Between the fall of the gauDa-s and the rise of the pAla-s there were smaller dynasties in the va~Nga country. One of them was the khaDga-s, who had names like: khaDgodyama, jAtakhaDga, devakhaDga and udIrNakhaDga. We believe they named themselves after the rhinoceros, which was common in va~Nga then. Their coins looked like cheap hacks of the gupta dInAra-s
