Chittorgarh Jauhar is the first and only jauhar of India but that's not true. 

1. Jauhar of Sindh ( 712CE) led by Queen Ladi , wife of King Dahir Sen of Sindh during the invasion of Muhammad bin Qasim, the first Islamic invader , who invaded India . 

2. Jauhar of Birsinnghapur, Bengal (1226 CE) , during the invasion of Ghiyasuddin Iwaz Shah. 

3. Jauhar of Gwalior (1232CE) , during the invasion of Shamsuddin Iltutmish. 

4. First jauhar of Jaisalmer (1299CE) , during the invasion of Allauddin Khilji, 24,000 women committed jauhar. 

5. Jauhar of Ranthambore (1301 CE) , during the invasion of Allauddin Khilji, jauhar led by Queen Ranga Devi. 

6. First jauhar of Chittorgarh (1303CE) , during the invasion of Allauddin Khilji, jauhar led by Queen Padmavati/ Padmini along with 16,000 women. 

7. Jauhar of Siwana ( 1308 CE) , invaded by Allauddin Khilji. 

8. Jauhar of Jalore (1311CE) , invaded by emperor of Khilji dynasty, jauhar led by Queens Jaitalde and Kamalade. 

9. Second jauhar of Jaisalmer (1326 CE) , invaded by Muhammad bin Tughlaq. 

10. Jauhar of Kampili, Karnataka ( 1327CE) , invaded by Muhammad bin Tughlaq. 

11. Jauhar of Bhatner (1398CE) , invaded by Islamic invader Timur. 

12 . Jauhar of Gagron Fort (1423CE) , invaded by Islamic invader Hoshang Shah. 

13. Jauhar of Chanderi (1528CE) , invaded by Mughal emperor Babur, jauhar led by Queen Manimala along with 1600 women. 

14. First jauhar of Raisen (1532CE) , invaded by Bahadur Shah, jauhar led by Queen Durgavati along with 700 women. 

15. Second jauhar of Chittorgarh (1535CE) , invaded by Bahadur Shah, jauhar led by Queen Karnavati along with 13,000 women. 

16. Second jauhar of Raisen (1543CE) , invaded by Sher Shah Suri. 

17. Third jauhar of Chittorgarh (1568CE) , invaded by Mughal emperor Akbar, jauhar led by Queen Phool Kanwar. 

18. Jauhar of Bundelkhand ( 1634CE) , invaded by Mughal emperor Aurangzeb. And many more
