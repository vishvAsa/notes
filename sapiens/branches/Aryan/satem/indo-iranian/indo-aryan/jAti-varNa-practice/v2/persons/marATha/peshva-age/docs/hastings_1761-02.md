In February 1761, after Panipat, and then the defeat of Shah Alam II by Carnac in Bihar, Warren Hastings at Calcutta wrote:

"The two great contending Powers at this time in this Empire are the Abdallees and the Marhattas; the one coming from the Borders of Persia, surrounded by fierce & hostile Neighbours, and governed by no fixed or steady Plan; the other situated in the Heart of ye Empire, and in Possession of the greatest part of it, united in a firm Establishment, and with Resources for ever at Hand. It must therefore be for our Interest at all Times to oppose the latter, when we can do it with Safety to ourselves, and there never appeared a more plausible occasion."
