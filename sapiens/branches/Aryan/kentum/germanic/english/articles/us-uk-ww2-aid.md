"Net US aid to the UK in the 1940s—chiefly Lend-Lease (about $25 billion after reverse aid, the bulk written off, with residual usable items sold at a 90% discount and rolled into the loan), the $3.75 billion Anglo-American Loan itself (repaid in later inflated dollars), and roughly $3 billion in Marshall Plan grants—formed an enormous real transfer. In today’s dollars that package exceeds $500 billion, making it the largest concentrated intergovernmental resource transfer to a single country in history."
