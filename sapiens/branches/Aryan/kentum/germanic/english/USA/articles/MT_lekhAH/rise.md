## Civil war roots
Source: [TW](https://x.com/blog_supplement/status/2105091262357323997)

I've often wondered when the American military edge over other nations began. Their civil war leaders, Grant and Sherman, looked more disorderly and dependent on superior numbers than their German coevals. However, over time, I've come to appreciate that they birthed modern warfare and clearly laid the foundation for the beginnings of the American hegemony. It is ironic that the civil war that split the American nation ended up training them to rise to be the world hegemon.
