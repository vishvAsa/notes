Paganism was made a capital offence for the Saxons by Charlemagne. Around 4,500 Saxon Pagans were massacred by Christian forces at Verden. Infants were forced to be baptised within a year of their birth, Charlemagne built churches at the Saxons expense and made eating meat during lent a capital offence among other restrictions.

however Charlemagne safeguarded the Jews, giving them royal protection and granting Jewish merchants the ability to travel freely and to conduct long distance commerce, privileges not extended to the native population.

He also allowed Jews to own Pagan slaves but not Christian slaves.
