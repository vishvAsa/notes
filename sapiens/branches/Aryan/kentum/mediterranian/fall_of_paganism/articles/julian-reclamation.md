Another example would be the Oracle of Apollo at Daphne - which had been 'disabled' in its function by the (Christian) Caesar Gallus, who had ordered the body of St Babylas interred thereat for precisely this purpose. This was to be undone by the Emperor Julian (Rome's last Pagan Emperor), who had the Christian saint disinterred & reconsecrated the site. Funnily enough, we actually have a Christian source mentioning that this indeed lead to a resumption of Oracular function. [text is footnote from Philostorgius, Amidon tr.]

Unfortunately, the place of Apollo "Mysteriously Burned Down" very soon after Julian's restoration of it. Julian made the obvious inference that the "Hand of God" responsible for it was ... a human one, attached (if only up until shortly after Julian caught up with them) to a mortal Christian - acting upon similar motive to, say, the Taliban at Bamiyan
