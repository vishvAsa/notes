Indian rock art from Matiyahi, Banda, UP. It is believed to be from the Mesolithic, though some might interpret the weapon as an OCP/Cu-hoard harpoon. Apart from the peculiar headdress of the hunter impaling a gaur, it is unique for showing his dog beside him. If this is indeed from the Mesolithic, these are depictions of people with Reich's "AASI" ancestry. This would show that they had hunting dogs with them. It is unclear if parts of the genome of this breed of dog have made it to modern Indian dogs.
