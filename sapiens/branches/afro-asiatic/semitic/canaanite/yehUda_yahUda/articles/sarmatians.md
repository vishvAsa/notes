The Israelite Samaritans are the direct continuous living descendants of the Ancient Jews who never left Israel over 3000 years. They are genetically identical to Bronze Age Israel. The Ancient Israelites survive in the modern Israelites and are the direct link of the nation of Israel to the Bronze Age. They speak an archaic dialect of Hebrew, have their own Torah and do not follow Rabbinical Judaism.

The Palestinian Muslims (and Christians) are mixed with Arabs and Normans, and are not natives.
