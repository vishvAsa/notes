Many successful people often overrate agency because they attribute things to their own acts. They tend to overlook or deny luck because that makes them feel less special. They forget the fact that it is a series of lotteries: 

1. the genes their parents passed on to them;  
2. the time and place where they were brought up and came to live.  
3. The people they associated with in the formative stages of their lives. 

A man who sees great success, failure and neglect gains a certain insight into this  
and, if well-endowed, might experience a sense of detachment.
