Source: [TW](https://x.com/blog_supplement/status/2104390929775804916)

As far as one can help it, it is better to watch great or brilliant people from afar. Getting too close to them of your own accord is like sampAti nearing the sun -- the result would probably be bad for you. But then there is always the temptation that by getting close to them, one might be able to avail of some reflected glory to enhance oneself.
