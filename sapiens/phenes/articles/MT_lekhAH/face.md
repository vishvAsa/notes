Reading faces is a useful skill to have. Early in school life, we learnt that the evil of some people can be easily read off their faces. One can be wary of such and anticipate that they are likely to mean/cause harm. 

But there are some psychopaths whose mischief or malice is hidden by an unusual face that many people interpret sympathetically. It is usually a face with some features that elicit pity in the beholder. 

However, hidden behind that face is a deep evil or, in the least, a tendency for harmful mischief. However, subtle evil cues can be caught in such a face, though it is often too late to act upon. We have ourselves fallen for such a face, much to our detriment.

The late fake Cambridge professor +++(→Jason Arday)+++ was one such example. A lot of people fall for his face and take it as a flag of innocence or someone deserving pity. However, the experienced reader can see that it has subtle clues of a psychopath. 

Mental illness does not mean a person cannot be damaging to society. However, containing such a psychopath is always difficult because he has a number of supporters who misread his demeanor and hold him to be innocent.
