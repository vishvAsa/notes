title = "Bronchodilator"
+++

-  relaxes and opens the airways, or bronchi, in the lungs.

## beta-2 adrenergic receptor agonist
- leads to relaxation of smooth muscle in the lung

- Theophylline	

### Long-acting 
- Formoterol - rapid - 1-3 min onset, 12hrs effectiveness.
  
- salmeterol (30-45 min onset, 12hrs effectiveness)
  -  + Fluticasone Propionate (a Gluco-corticoid anti-inflammatory)- Seroflo 250 inhaler
- Tiotropium

### Short-acting
- Aka rescue inhalers.
- Salbutamol, albuterol
- Asthalin
