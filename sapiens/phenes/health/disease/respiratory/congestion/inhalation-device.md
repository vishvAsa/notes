## Environment friendliness
rotocaps (Dry Powder Inhalers / DPIs) are significantly more environmentally friendly than traditional pressurized metered-dose inhaler (pMDI) canisters and Synchrobreathe devices.

A single pMDI canister can have a carbon footprint equivalent to driving a car for up to 175–200 kilometers (around 15 to 30 kg of $\text{CO}_2$ equivalent per device).

The carbon footprint of a DPI/rotocap device is typically less than 1/20th (up to 95% lower) of a standard pMDI canister.

## Capsule powders
Requires a quick, deep inhalation effort

Branding and (size?) differences -  
rotacaps for rotahalers, transcaps for transhalers, redicaps

Dry powder inhalers are highly engineered. The internal shape of the Rotahaler creates a specific vortex to lift the medication out of a Rotacap and break the powder down into microscopic particles suitable for your lungs.  
Transcaps are formulated for the airflow resistance of their specific proprietary device. Mixing them can mean the powder clumps or doesn't reach deep enough into your airways.

One can manually open the capsule, pour the powder in any inhaler and get the job done.
