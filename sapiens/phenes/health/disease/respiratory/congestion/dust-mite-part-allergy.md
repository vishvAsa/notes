## Allergens
Dust mites themselves are 100-300 microns, and the allergy-causing fecal pellets are 10 to 40 microns. 

## Bedding
### Cover
Medical reference: a woven cover with a pore size of 2 to 10 μm can prevent passage of house dust mites. Anything under 10 microns should block even the smallest dust particles. 

### Washing
Other low-cost steps that help more than the cover material  

- Wash all bedding weekly at 60C, dry in sun
- Vacuum mattress with HEPA vacuum weekly
- Keep indoor humidity <50% if possible, use cross-ventilation
- No carpets, heavy curtains, or stuffed toys in bedroom
- Wash pillow itself every 2-3 months

#### Terry + TPU
Terry + TPU waterproof protector with zipper  
This is what most Flipkart / Meesho listings sell as "100% waterproof dust mite protector". The TPU membrane inside has pores measured around ∼0.16 microns - so also 0 barrier but it is slightly breathable and doesn't feel like plastic.  
Price: Rs 600-1200 for single, Rs 800-1500 for queen on Flipkart/Meesho. Search "zippered mattress encasement dust mite proof".

### Non-breathable ones
0 microns. Very hot. Mold loves humidity.
Seams leak: Rexin sheets stitched at home have needle holes. Mites come in through the stitch holes and zipper.
