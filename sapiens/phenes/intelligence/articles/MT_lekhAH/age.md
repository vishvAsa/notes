An elderly relative of mine recently passed away. From an early age, he had considerable mathematical capacity. A year before his death in the upper 80s, he put out a paper with involved mathematics in the realm of QCD. He was an example of a person who was able to retain his mental faculties through physical decline and soldier on to get a paper out before dying. It reinforced to me the point that some people somehow retain their mental capacity and drive into old age.

Other people have explosive capacity in their youth, but it fades in their 50s. Yet others retain their mental capacity but have some kind of a burnout or loss of drive. I think drive is the key factor. If people have the drive, then they perform abhyAsa. This abhyAsa helps maintain their mental faculties unless they are struck by a neurological disease. Drive, in turn, might depend on extrinsic motivation. If people don't get that, they might lose drive. Very few people can go with intrinsic motivation, though it is common for people to claim they are doing something only niShkAmya.
