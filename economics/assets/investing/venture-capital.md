These are risky investments in startups.  

## Ponzi scheme mode
Early investors boost the project by demanding that the entrepreneur show potential by increasing revenue "100% in a month" while burning money.  
When newer investors are attracted to invest at a higher valuation, the early investor exits.  
This cycle can continue a few times - till finally the IPO.  
The IPO buyers are the final loosers when the business collapses.
