A lot of 27yos are leaving genuinely compounding careers in PE, tech or consulting to start a generic agency, newsletter or micro-SaaS making $70k because some guy in Bali convinced them they were a “slave to the matrix”.

Truth is most will probably never catch up financially with the people who stayed in a compounding field.

Obviously money isn’t everything. People leave for autonomy, a shot at the right tail and probably a more interesting life. 

The scam is that most solopreneur businesses don’t actually offer much of a right tail.

And the whole escape industry has made a speciality of hiding that by humiliating employment. Sarcastic 9-5 memes. Screenshots from Bali. The same five 0.1% winners recycled across every podcast. A course seller explaining that your boss owns your life, before selling you a business where 14 clients own it instead.

They can’t present self-employment as one reasonable option among many. Your current life has to feel intolerable or you won’t buy the course, community or newsletter teaching you how to escape it.

Then you quit and discover there’s no distribution, valuable IP or ownership that compounds. Just constant lead generation and an income that disappears when you stop working/posting.

You haven’t made an asymmetric bet. You’ve exchanged a compounding career for a business with venture risk but freelance-level returns.
