Ancient Indians Discussing Magnetic Field

In Tattvasaṅgraha, Śāntarakṣita quotes mata of a (sambhavataḥ tīrthika) saiddhāntika ācārya named Śaṅkarasvāmi and dismisses it. Śaṅkarasvāmi's mata has aucitya for vartamānaloka, who have been avagata with sāmānyasiddhānta-s of bhautikī (physics). Mūḍha-s are those who say, out of their ajñātā & vismṛti, that the mata of ayaskāntīyakṣetra (magnetic field) didn't exist among ārya-s before the vāda-s of Faraday, Ørsted, etc. gained prasiddhi. Although not all saiddhāntika-s did grahaṇa of such a mata due to dārśanika bheda-s, it was prajñāta among budha-s in a non-mathematical vikasita rūpa.

[1/5]
Śāntarakṣita's Tattvasaṅgraha (2519):

If it's said (in pūrvapakṣa) that– "there is ākarṣaṇa by ayaskānta (cumbaka) surely due to (sparśa of the loha, etc. dhātu with the) prabhā (utpanna from cumbaka)"–then (the siddhānta is), as no such prabhā is dṛśya, how can its astitva be jñāta?

Kamalaśīla's Tattvasaṅgraha-pañjikā:

In 'ayaskāntaprabhā...', ācārya (Śāntarakṣita) expresses an āśaṅkā regarding siddhi of the dṛṣṭānta on the basis of Śaṅkarasvāmi's mata, who argues – "The ākarṣaṇa of ayas (loha) is due to being vedhita by the prabhā udbhūta from the ayaskānta (cumbaka), for if it were not so, then there would be ākarṣaṇa of ayas (loha) sthita in all deśas. Even though this prabhā isn't upalabhya like prabhā of a pradīpa, still it's anumeya because of the darśana of such ākarṣaṇa even when there's vyavadhāna or at dūrī (distance)." By 'nanu...', the ācārya (Śāntarakṣita) does khaṇḍana by saying as this isn't dṛśya, the niṣedha of its sadvyavahāra is done on grounds of its anupalabdhi.

[2/5]Image
So, as we see, ācārya Śaṅkarasvāmi states:

• There's a prabhā udbhūta from an ayaskānta.
• The ākarṣaṇa of ayas to the ayaskānta is due to it being vedhita by the ayaskānta's prbhā.
• If there was abhāva of such a prabhā then there would be ākarṣaṇa of ayas, sthita in all deśa-s, to the ayaskānta. It has niyata-deśa-vartitva, because only ayas within a niyata deśa are ākarṣita.
• Although this prabhā isn't upalabhya like the prabhā of pradīpa (which is spaṣṭa dṛṣya), still on the grounds of anumāna its bhāva is siddha because the ākarṣaṇa of ayas to ayaskānta occurs even when there is vyavadhāna or viprakarṣa (in deśa).

Śāntarakṣita's argument, that just because prabhā isn't dṛṣya, its astitva is bhrāmaka, is just lame. However, Śāntarakṣita gives a successive argument.


Śāntarakṣita's Tattvasaṅgraha (2520):

Whence is it āpta that this prabhā doesn't do samākarṣaṇa of kāṣṭha, etc. If it's argued that– "it does so (ākarṣaṇa of only ayas) due to the niyatatva of its śakti", then same may be argued even in the case aprāpti (asaṁyoga).

Kamalaśīla's Tattvasaṅgrahapañjikā:

Moreover, as for the prasaṅga-sādhana ukta by the pūrvapakṣavādin: "Because of the prasaṅga of ākarṣaṇa of ayasa avasthita in all deśa-s", ācārya (Śāntarakṣita) doing parihāra of that says "Whence is it āpta..." and so on. Even in the pakṣa of prāpti, the prasaṅga is tulya: Why does that ayaskānta prabhā having prāpta every vastu such as kāṣṭha, etc. not do their samākarṣaṇa? If it's argued (by pūrvapakṣavādin) that: "There's no atiprasaṅga due to pratiniyamatā of the padārtha's svabhāva". Then it's stated (in siddhānta) that: " Even in the pakṣa of aprāpti, by whom has the niyamakatva of the padārtha's svabhāva been apahṛta, by virtue of which the same is not iṣṭa in this case?". Thus, the kalpanā of an adṛṣṭa prabhā is clearly vyartha.

[4/5]Image
Śāntarakṣita's uttara-khaṇḍana is stated as follows:

• The Pūrvapakṣin states that without saṁparka with ayaskānta's prabhā, ayas sthita in all deśa-s would be ākarṣita.
• Since this ayaskānta's prabhā is prasārita through deśa then it does saṁparka with kāṣṭha, etc. as well. So, why aren't those kāṣṭha, etc. ākarṣita to ayaskānta?
• If the Pūrvapakṣin argues that it's so because of the niyatatva of ayaskānta's svabhāva, then such holds even in the case of aprāpti with ayaskānta's prabhā.
• If the Pūrvapakṣin argues that it's so because of the niyatatva of ayaskānta's svabhāva, then such holds even in the case of aprāpti with ayaskānta's prabhā.
• Since ayaskānta's svabhāva/śakti is the nimitta of ākarṣaṇa itself, then what's the point in doing kalpanā of an adṛṣṭa prabhā of ayaskānta?
