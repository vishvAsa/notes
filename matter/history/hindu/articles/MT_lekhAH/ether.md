The connection between electricity, magnetism and light was realized within a 20-year window in the 1800s when the H were going through their darkest hour:
1. 1846, Michael Faraday: Performs experiment showing plane of polarization of light is rotated by a magnetic field; far-reachingly hypothesizes that light is a transverse vibration of electric and magnetic lines of force.
2. 1856, Wilhelm Weber and Rudolf Kohlrausch: Electrostatic Units (ESU): defined based on the static force between stationary charges. Electromagnetic Units (EMU): Defined by measuring the magnetic force between currents. Charge(ESU)/Charge(EMU) = c
3. 1857, Gustav Kirchhoff: differential equations for electrical signals moving through a zero-resistance wire show that electricity propagates as a wave with speed c
4. 1858, Bernhard Riemann: the propagation velocity of signal between two separated electric charges is c
5. 1865, James Clerk Maxwell: unifies Faraday's theory with the Weber and Kirchhoff's formulations, deriving the electromagnetic wave equation establishing that light is an electromagnetic wave.

However, Maxwell's conception of electromagnetism was not the same as it is today. He believed that there was a mysterious substance termed aether in which spinning vortices carried magnetism while spherical particles acting as "ball-bearings" between these vortices carried electricity. Thus he stated: "We can scarcely avoid the conclusion that light consists in the transverse undulations of the same medium which is the cause of electric and magnetic phenomena."

This mechanical view of the force field as being mediated by an "aetherial fluid" has a deep history in human thought. It can be compared to the old Hindu view that celestial bodies were bound to each other by "vāta-raśanā or vāta-rajju", literally wind-ropes. These were in principle conceived as mechanical transmitters of gravity. Truly modern physics originated only when Einstein conclusively killed the mechanical aether. But in a sense, a different type of aether has come back in quantum field theory, where all space is permeated with fields like fermions, bosons and scalar (Higgs), and force carriers and particles are excitations of those fields. Quantum vacuum is the ground state of all these fields – a roiling aether at the microscopic level with particles constantly forming and disappearing.
