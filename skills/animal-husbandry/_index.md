## Tax status
Proceeds taxable in India, unlike cultivation.  
Capital gains from sales not taxable upto 2 cows/ goats etc., if kept for family needs. (Horse not included)

## Butchering age
Animal meat gets tougher with age.  
So, naturally dead animal's meat is likely poor.  
Different species have different butchering ages.
