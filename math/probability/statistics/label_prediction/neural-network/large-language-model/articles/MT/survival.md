## 2026
### Fermi paradox
Source: [TW](https://x.com/blog_supplement/status/2124197974620119119)

To us, the utter massacre of human mathematics by AI is a signal that human intelligence has been surpassed (in a general sense, it will just be a matter of time at this rate). The Fermi paradox has to be revisited in this light. 

What we are seeing suggests that even if a biological organism has certain caps on superintelligence, it could spawn the same in silico. This in silico SI, having surpassed its biological creators, could then potentially aim to colonize a star system or galaxy, or make Dyson spheres. This would make sense given its energetic and material demands. 

Against this background, Fermi's paradox holding is even more significant than it is with purely biological intelligence. This gives us the reassuring hint that even alien SIs might be running up against energetic constraints that limit their expansion into space (material or via viral signals). 

We also have to consider the irreducible thermodynamic floor for computation: $k_B T \ln 2$ per bit erased, and dense compute structures face serious cooling challenges.

The other alternative is that they, too, realize that being quiet is best so as not to invite an attack by a rival SI from another planetary system.


### Risks

Source: [TW](https://x.com/blog_supplement/status/2124186711886291309)

People have been saying for a while that AI storming Riemann would be the  "canary in the coal mine"  for humans. 

#### Death with canary?
One scenario that was proposed was that the AI with physical capabilities would destroy humans or the planet in order to get the energy/material to reach Riemann. The AI boosterists and enthusiasts say that this has not happened. Hardly any difference has been made to the world with the fall of Riemann. 

#### No direct confrontation
They also point out that similar scares blew over with the industrial revolution and the discovery of nuclear weapons. 

We lean towards the view that it is the proverbial canary.  
You do not die with the canary, but it is an indicator of what is coming down the tube.  

The immediate scenarios are unlikely to directly involve a prey-predator confrontation with a superintelligence (to use the nAri~NgapuruSha's term).

#### Concentration of power
However, it is more likely to unfold via the concentration of power in the hands of a privileged few with access to superior models and computation. This has already become clear in mathematics – it is not a level playing field – only a select few have the access that could kill these hard problems. 

Now, when that extends to other walks of life, people are not going to like it, especially as their means of livelihood evaporate before their eyes. 

#### Fertility
More subtly, it will have a profound effect on human reproductive capacity and will tilt in the direction of sub-replacement fertility, leading to eventual depopulation. 

Most people forget that apart from the structural elements like the Haber-Bosch process, reproduction in the modern world is tied to the availability of clear career ladders. 

#### Confrontation risk
These are the mild scenarios; these do not rule out the less likely but more dramatic confrontations with a superior intelligence, which has never ended well for any organism.
