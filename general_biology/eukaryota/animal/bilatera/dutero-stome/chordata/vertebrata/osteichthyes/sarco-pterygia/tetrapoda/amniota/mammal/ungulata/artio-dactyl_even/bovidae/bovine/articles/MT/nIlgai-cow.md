> During Akbar’s reign, a debate broke out between the Rajput nobles regarding the genus of the nilgai and whether it was permissible to eat it. Raja Man Singh believed it to be a kind of a cow but it was finally agreed that the nilgai belonged to the family of deer.


The Nilgai is closer to cows than to deer in the biological sense. It is only a sister group of the core cow clade (Bovini) rather than being within it. Its closest cousin is the chousinga
