## Ape hunting
Source: [TW](https://x.com/blog_supplement/status/2123657909208858806)

Death by Dinosaur: There is a recent study showing that Verreaux's eagles hunt baboons considerably heavier than themselves. The record is a 26.2 kg baboon killed by such an eagle. A brutal death is dealt by puncturing the skull through the eye and piercing the brain. This supports Berger's hypothesis that our ancestors, like the australopithecines, were killed and eaten by eagles. This could be extrapolated to the Mesozoic, where theropods hunted sauropods considerably larger than themselves.
https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/epdf/10.1002/ajpa.70377


## Early footprints
Source: [TW](https://x.com/blog_supplement/status/1928684371860504762)

We know hardly anything about the early dinosaurs: depending on how one interprets footprints from what is today Poland, they were possibly present as early as 250 million years ago and almost certainly by 246 MYA. Yet, barring the fragmentary Nyasasaurus (just a right humerus and a few vertebrae), skeletal remains appear only ~230 MYA. So the first 15-20 million years of Dinosaur history is a blackhole.
