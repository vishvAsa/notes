The final period of the Age of the Reptiles, the Cretaceous Period, lasted 79 million years. That is more than the entire Age of the Mammals, which has so far run for about 66 My. There were birds throughout the Cretaceous. They mostly came in two great types: the toothed and hand-clawed Enantiornithines and the Euornithines. All modern birds are a single branch of Euornithines. All Enantiornithines became extinct at the end of the Cretaceous, along with other dinosaurs, even though they were the dominant type of bird in the Cretaceous. They used to fly shortly after hatching, but their cerebral hemisphere were much less developed than modern Euornithines. TThus, for a long time, birds ran off a much leaner brain. A secular increase in intelligence is not a given in evolution.
