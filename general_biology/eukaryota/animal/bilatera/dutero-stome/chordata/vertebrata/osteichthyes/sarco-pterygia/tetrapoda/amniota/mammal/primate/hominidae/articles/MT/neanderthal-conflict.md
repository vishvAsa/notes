## Spear fight
Source: [TW](https://x.com/blog_supplement/status/2110964358259908875)

Direct evidence for a fatal spear fight among Neanderthals in West Asia:
Around 75K years ago an older Neanderthal (~45 years of age) who had his face bashed in an earlier conflict and survived finally met his end at the business end of a spear. His assailant seems to have deliberately targeted his heart, as the spear went right through it, leaving a hole in the scapula. Since there are only Neanderthals at this site, it was like N-on-N violence rather than sapiens being involved.
