## Poison
Source: [TW](https://x.com/blog_supplement/status/2121095271689900142)

H. sapiens from around 125K years ago in Arabia were using arrows poisoned with Acokanthera schimperi, which produces the toxic steroid Ouabain. It suggests that this African toxin, used in warfare and hunting, probably has deep roots in the Paleolithic era.

But long before humans, the African crested rat had already discovered the use of this toxin. It chews on the roots and bark of this tree and obtains a paste with this toxin, which it then spreads on its fur. Special wick hairs take it up, and it can cause a heart attack in predators
